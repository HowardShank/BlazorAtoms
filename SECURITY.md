# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

Each `BlazorAtoms.*` package is versioned independently. Security fixes are made against the
latest published version of each package; older versions may or may not be patched. (See Warranty section below.)

Security fixes, if any, are provided at the discretion of the maintainers.

This policy does not create any obligation to provide support, maintenance services, SLAs, custom fixes, hosted services, managed services, operational monitoring, professional services, consulting, or certification of customer products.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.

Report it privately via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/HowardShank/BlazorAtoms/security/advisories/new)
for this repository, or contact [@HowardShank](https://github.com/HowardShank) directly.

Include:

- Which package(s) and version(s) are affected
- Steps to reproduce, or a minimal repro
- The potential impact

You should get an acknowledgment within a few days. If confirmed, a fix will be prepared and a
new package version published; you'll be credited in the advisory unless you ask not to be.

## Scope
This policy applies only to security vulnerabilities in BlazorAtoms libraries themselves.

This policy does not apply to:

customer applications
customer products
customer deployments
customer infrastructure
customer data
third-party services
unsupported versions
end-of-life versions
forks or modified versions
usage outside the documented or intended behavior of the relevant library
Organizations using BlazorAtoms libraries are responsible for assessing, securing, testing, monitoring, updating, and maintaining their own applications, products, deployments, infrastructure, and supply chains.


## Cyber Resilience Act
(Needs review and approval)

Open-source software (OSS) is generally exempt unless monetized or systematically supported for commercial purposes. OSS stewards are introduced as a regulatory category, required to maintain cybersecurity policies and vulnerability reporting without direct fines for CRA violations.

BlazorAtoms libraries are general-purpose software libraries.

They are not cybersecurity products, identity or access management systems, password managers, operating systems, browsers, firewalls, network management tools, SIEM tools, hypervisors, container runtimes, or other Cyber Resilience Act important or critical product classes.

If a BlazorAtoms library is treated as a product with digital elements under the Cyber Resilience Act, BlazorAtoms assesses it as an ordinary software component.

Organizations incorporating BlazorAtoms libraries into products made available on the EU market are responsible for assessing and meeting their own regulatory obligations for those products, including any obligations under the Cyber Resilience Act.

BlazorAtoms does not provide support, maintenance services, SLAs, managed services, hosted services, operational monitoring, custom fixes, professional services, consulting, or certification of customer products.

Security vulnerabilities in supported BlazorAtoms libraries are handled through the GitHub Security Advisory process for the relevant repository, where appropriate.

From 11 September 2026, if BlazorAtoms becomes aware of credible active exploitation of a vulnerability in a supported BlazorAtoms library, or a severe security incident affecting a supported BlazorAtoms library, BlazorAtoms may report the matter through the applicable Cyber Resilience Act reporting mechanism where legally required.
